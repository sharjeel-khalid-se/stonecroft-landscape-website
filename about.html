Craftsmanship Born From the Northeast Earth

For nearly two decades, we have shaped private grounds that balance the permanence of native stone with the soft, ever-shifting moods of regional flora.

Our Heritage

Founded on an unwillingness to cut corners.

Stonecroft began in 2008 when licensed landscape architect Julian Vance partnered with master stonecutter Cormac Ward. Both had grown weary of seeing expensive landscapes fail after three seasons due to inadequate sub-bases, hurried plant installation, and poor soil preparation.

They envisioned a firm where design and execution were unified under one roof. No passing the buck to detached subcontractors. No substituting cheap nursery closeouts for healthy specimen trees. And no laying pavers over loose dust that sinks during the first hard winter freeze.

Today, Stonecroft operates a multidisciplinary studio and stone yard in Bedford, NY. Our full-time staff includes registered landscape architects, ISA-certified arborists, master stone masons, and specialized horticultural crews.

Our Principles

How We Distinguish Our Craft

A high-end landscape requires more than aesthetic flair; it demands geological understanding and deep botanical respect.

Geological Authenticity

We source quarried stone indigenous to our Appalachian and Hudson Valley geology. Natural stone belongs here and weathers into the forest margin with timeless elegance.

Subsurface Engineering

A terrace is only as durable as what lies beneath. We dig below the regional frost line (36â€“42 inches), build with multi-layer crushed stone aggregate, and install robust geotextile drainage fabrics.

Biological Soil Vitality

Plants fail when soil is dead. We reject synthetic salt fertilizers that kill beneficial mycorrhizae. Instead, we condition all planting pits with mature organic compost, kelp meal, and beneficial fungi.

Generational Durability

We build our masonry walls and select tree varieties with the next thirty years in mind. When you invest in a Stonecroft project, you are creating an enduring legacy for your family property.
