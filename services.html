Frequently Asked Questions

Working with Stonecroft

What is your typical project scope and budget range?

Our comprehensive landscape architecture and construction projects typically range from $25,000 for focused terrace or garden installations up to $350,000+ for multi-acre estate master plans and pool environments. For seasonal grounds stewardship, our monthly service contracts begin at $850/month.

How do you handle permitting and town approvals?

We manage the entire municipal process in-house. Our landscape architects prepare detailed architectural submissions, soil erosion plans, and stormwater calculations for town planning boards and wetland commissions across Westchester and Fairfield counties.

Do you use subcontractors or your own employees?

All core masonry, stone cutting, grading, planting, and horticultural care is performed by our full-time, insured craftsmen and crew chiefs. For specialized sub-trades such as gunite swimming pool shells or high-voltage utility hookups, we coordinate directly with our vetted master partners.

What guarantees do you offer on plant material and stonework?

We provide a full two-year warranty on all installed nursery stock when paired with our estate maintenance program, and an industry-leading five-year structural guarantee on all masonry foundations and retaining wall installations.
